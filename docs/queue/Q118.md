# Pre-consolidation rows were moved into the store, not rewritten for it

The 2026-08-22 consolidation gave every item its own file. It did not rewrite
the items themselves, so thirteen rows still carry what a `docs/STATUS.md` Notes
cell could hold -- the layout's ~250-character cap -- in a store whose whole
point is that the body has no cap and the item owns a page.

The split is total. Measured 2026-08-23 over all thirty rows, counting body
words:

| | rows | words |
|---|---|---|
| filed at or below Q91 | 13 | 29 -- 48 |
| filed at or above Q92 | 17 | 158 -- 659 |

No row falls between 48 and 158. Every terse row predates the cutover and every
scoped row postdates it, with no overlap and no exception -- the boundary is the
consolidation itself rather than any property of the work.

Six of the thirteen are `ready`: Q74, Q82, Q84, Q85, Q87, Q88 -- Q91 was the
seventh until it shipped. The other six are deferred, where a one-line trigger is
the correct form and this does not reach them.

**What it costs is selection, not storage.** Those six have been passed over
by every batch since the cutover, and the ID gap that would normally read as
staleness cannot say why here -- the consolidation renumbered six of them, so
the gap measures the renumber as much as any turnover. What is measurable is
what they are missing. The skill's scoping pass asks four questions, and a row
of forty words answers none of them: who it is for, what happens if it is never
built, which way it gets built, and how big it is on that approach. Q84 is the
shape -- two sentences, a prescription from `hook-verdict`, and no statement of
who is worse off today. A worker handed it has to reconstruct the case before it
can start, and a groomer ranking it has only cost to rank on.

So this is not a prune. Every one of the seven was spot-checked against the tree
in the same groom and every premise held: Q82's fixture counts re-measured at 35
/ 6 / 6 exactly as written, and Q84's `supervise` posture is still absent from
`plugins/workspace-guard/scripts/bash-workspace-guard.py`, which reports zero
occurrences of `POSTURE`. They describe real gaps in a form nothing can pick up.

The work is six scoping passes, one per row, each rewriting its own file and
naming the rank it then deserves. That is `M` as a body of work and `S` per row,
and it parallelizes cleanly because a scoping pass touches one file. Deletion
stays on the table for any individual row once it has been scoped -- scoping is
the step allowed to decide an item should not exist -- but deciding that now,
from the forty words, is the reason they are stuck rather than a way out.
