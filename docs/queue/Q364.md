# The release-note workflow still fails open through `if: false`

`test_the_check_fails_closed` (Q243) pins the gating line and the absence of
`continue-on-error`. The independent review of #335 (review-r3, at `879b344`,
https://github.com/karlkfi/claude-bouncer/pull/335#issuecomment-6937758198)
reported two more mutants that stay green: `if: false` on the release-note
step, and `if: false` on the job. On the job it gives a skipped check, which
reads as a pass in the checks list and which the workflow's own comment says
this repo does not spend. On the step the job still reports green while the
check never ran, which is the same blindness by another route. Not re-run by
the filer; the reviewer's reading, to be reproduced first.

## Which way it gets built

Assert that `release-note.yml` carries no `if:` key at job or step level, and
that an `exit 1` follows the gate line inside the same `run:` block. Drive both
mutants and the missing-`exit 1` mutant red, as Q243 did for its four.

## Who is worse off

The repository's pull-request gate: a workflow edit that skips the step passes
every local and CI check, and the check reports success on bodies it never
judged. Same reach as Q243; flat cost, met only when someone edits that
workflow.
