# main reads red when no macOS runner takes the launcher job

On 2026-11-17, 5 of 12 `tests` runs on `main` failed for one reason:
`exit-status-guard-launcher (macos-latest)` was cancelled about 15 minutes
after it was created, with no step run and no runner assigned (`runner_name`
empty in the jobs API). The runs were 44110015736 (`ef725f10`), 44123234776
(`6908336d`), 44130458470 (`4ea1d077`), 44134370839 (`3bfd4eed`) and
44140018937 (`328f612d`). Every other job in those runs passed. When a runner
does take the job it finishes in 7 to 11 seconds (`f54f6053`, `ce774a8c`), so
the wait is for a runner and not for the job.

The harm is that `main` turns red on code nobody broke. The release process
tags a green `main`, and a red that is usually infrastructure teaches the
reader to stop believing red.

`timeout-minutes` does not reach this: it bounds a job's execution, and these
jobs never started. Which cancellation fired at 15 minutes is unmeasured, and
measuring it comes first. Candidates for the fix, once the cause is known: a
workflow that re-runs only a job cancelled with no runner assigned, or moving
the macOS leg to a runner label with more capacity.

Touches `.github/workflows/`, so the fix goes to Karl under the merge-posture
floor whatever the route says.
